# SOP-OPS-03: Shop Floor Incident Escalation Directory & Communication Protocol

## 1. Incident Escalation Overview
When unrecoverable defects, repeated machining variances, or critical safety/quality non-conformances are detected on the production line, operators must follow the formal tiered escalation hierarchy.

## 2. Quality Escalation Matrix (Cracks and Batch Defects)
For any structural crack, micro-fractures, cracks, metallurgical voids, or batch-wide defect trends (> 2 consecutive parts failing tolerance checks), line operators must immediately escalate to Quality Lead Marcus Vance at extension 4402 (Radio Channel 3, Pager ID: #QV-99). The Quality Lead is responsible for inspecting the lot, issuing a Stop-Ship notice if necessary, and approving the formal scrap ticket.

## 3. Maintenance Escalation Matrix (Thermal Drift & Fixture Wear)
For recurring dimensional deviations or CNC spindle thermal drift exceeding 0.05 mm across successive cuts, escalate immediately to Maintenance Lead Priya Nair at extension 4190 (Radio Channel 7). Maintenance will perform automated Renishaw probe calibration and laser alignment before line re-qualification.

## 4. Shift Supervision & Operations Escalation
For traveler discrepancies, missing work order documentation, or unresolved routing bottlenecks at Station B-4, contact Shift Operations Supervisor Elena Rostova at extension 4105.
