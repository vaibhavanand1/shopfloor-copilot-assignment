# SOP-ENG-01: Precision Machining & Molding Tolerance Specifications

## 1. Scope and Classification
This standard operating procedure governs surface finishes, structural defect thresholds, and dimensional allowances for all 6061-T6 aluminum machined parts and injection-molded precision components produced across Milling Lines 1 through 6.

## 2. Surface Scratch Tolerance Criteria
Surface scratches with a measured depth of less than 0.5 mm on non-mating cosmetic surfaces are classified as acceptable pending minor secondary rework. Scratches exceeding 0.5 mm in depth or any scratches situated on critical O-ring sealing grooves, bearing seats, or primary mating flanges represent immediate out-of-spec conditions and must be rejected.

## 3. Structural Crack Tolerance Limits
Cracks, micro-fractures, thermal stress fissures, and material tears of any measurable length or depth possess an absolute zero-tolerance threshold (0.0 mm tolerance). No crack can be repaired, welded, or blended under any operating condition. Any part presenting visual or dye-penetrant evidence of a crack must be removed from the machining fixture immediately.

## 4. Dimensional Deviation Allowances
Linear dimensions, bore diameters, and profile geometries are inspected against engineering drawing CAD models with a standard bilaterial tolerance of +/- 0.1 mm. A measured dimensional deviation greater than 0.2 mm from nominal CAD constitutes a critical QC failure and mandates immediate spindle pause.
